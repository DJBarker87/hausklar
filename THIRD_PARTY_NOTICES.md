# Third-party notices

HausKlar depends on open-source Python, npm, and Swift packages whose licences remain their own. Direct dependencies are declared in `server/pyproject.toml`, `server/web/package.json`, and `ios/HausKlar/project.yml`; lockfiles contain the resolved transitive set. This notice is not an exhaustive legal opinion. Distributors should regenerate a dependency licence report for the exact release and preserve notices required by bundled dependencies and assets.
